Turn saved posts into places you actually visit.

You find a great coffee shop on Instagram, a taco spot on TikTok, a trail on Facebook… and then never find it again. LocalSpots saves the place, not just the post, and nudges you when you’re close.

How it works

Share. Confirm. Go.

Three taps from a post you love to a spot on your map.

1

Share it to LocalSpots

In Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, tap Share and choose Save to LocalSpots. From Google Maps or Apple Maps, share the place the same way.

2

Confirm the place

LocalSpots reads the post’s caption when it’s public and suggests searches from it—📍 lines, quoted names, hashtags. Pick the right match, add a note and tags.

3

Find it when it matters

Your list is sorted by distance from you, and every spot opens straight in Google Maps so you can save it to your own lists there.

Good to know

Honest limits.

You confirm the place

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook don’t share a post’s location with apps, so LocalSpots helps you find it from the caption—and you tap to confirm.

Captions aren’t guaranteed

Public posts usually work. Private posts or login walls (Facebook especially) may mean typing the place name yourself.

Alerts watch your closest spots

iPhones let an app monitor 20 places at once, so LocalSpots watches the closest ones and re-points as you move. Alerts need “Always” location access, which is optional.

Privacy

Your places are yours.

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your saved spots stay on your iPhone (and in your own iCloud if you use Pro), and your location never leaves your device.
