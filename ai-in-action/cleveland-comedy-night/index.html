AI in Action · Travel & entertainment

A comedy night in Cleveland, booked from 30,000 feet.

I asked an AI assistant what to do on my trip. It came back with one of my favorite comedians and a ticket.

October 26, 2026 · On a flight from home to Cleveland · Tool: Muse, a Meta product

The ask

I was on a flight to Cleveland and had a free evening ahead of me, with no plans. So I asked Muse whether there was a comedy show I could catch while I was in town.

What Muse did

It didn’t just hand me a list of venues. It found a show at Hilarities in Cleveland with Nick Swardson, one of my favorite comedians. It suggested a time that worked for my trip, and then it booked the tickets for me.

I went from “I wonder if there’s anything on” to having tickets in hand before the plane landed.

Why it worked

  • I asked for an outcome, not information. “Can I see a comedy show?” is a different request from “list comedy clubs in Cleveland.”
  • It handled the whole chain. Searching, picking a time and booking all happened in one conversation.
  • It matched my taste. The show it found was someone I would have picked myself.

Try it yourself

Next time you’re traveling, tell your AI assistant the city, the dates and the kind of night you want, whether that’s comedy, live music or a good dinner. Let it search and suggest, and check the details before you confirm anything that costs money.
