DoseWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

iPhone app

The DoseWise iOS app stores everything you enter (peptides, mixes, dose logs, injection sites) only on your device. It has no accounts, makes no network requests, contains no analytics or advertising, and does not collect, share or sell any data. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Web app

The web app at dosewise.white-oak-ai.com keeps your data in your browser's local storage by default. If you choose to sign in, your library and dose history are synced to our cloud database (hosted by Supabase) so you can access them on other devices. Your data is used only to provide that sync; we do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for tracking. You can delete your data from within the app or by contacting us, and we will remove your account and its data.

Not medical advice

DoseWise is a calculator and log for reference only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Verify your numbers with a qualified healthcare professional.

Children

DoseWise is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: hello@white-oak-ai.com.
