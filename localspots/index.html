An iPhone app from White Oak AI

Turn saved posts into places you actually visit.

You find a great coffee shop on Instagram, a taco spot on TikTok, a trail on Facebook… and then never find it again. LocalSpots AI saves the place, not just the post, and nudges you when you’re close.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone
How it works

Share. Confirm. Go.

Three taps from a post you love to a spot on your map.

1

Share it to LocalSpots AI

In Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, tap Share and choose Save to LocalSpots AI. From Google Maps or Apple Maps, share the place the same way.

2

Confirm the place

LocalSpots AI reads the post’s caption when it’s public, and the text in its preview image, and suggests searches—📍 lines, quoted names, hashtags, names on screen. Pick the right match, add a note and tags.

3

Find it when it matters

Your list is sorted by distance from you, and every spot opens straight in Google Maps so you can save it to your own lists there.

Features

Built for people who collect places.

Saves from the apps you already use

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Google Maps and Apple Maps. A shared Maps place is ready to save in one tap.

On-device AI finds the place

It reads the text in a post’s image, and on iPhones with Apple Intelligence it offers a best guess for the place. It all runs on your iPhone; nothing is uploaded.

Sorted by what’s near you

See your saved places closest-first, or limit the list to the next 25 miles.

“You’re near Café Moka”

Optional nearby alerts ping you when you walk past a saved spot—at most once every six hours per place.

Plan a trip

Going somewhere? Add the trip and its dates, pick the saved spots near your destination you don’t want to miss, and check them off as you go.

Tags and notes

Label spots coffee, brunch, date night and filter your list with one tap.

Sync across your devices

With Pro, your list follows you through your own iCloud—no extra account to create.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your list lives on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Good to know

Honest limits.

You confirm the place

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook don’t share a post’s location with apps, so LocalSpots AI helps you find it from the caption and the post’s image—and you tap to confirm.

Captions aren’t guaranteed

Public posts usually work. Private posts or login walls (Facebook especially) may mean typing the place name yourself.

Alerts watch your closest spots

iPhones let an app monitor 20 places at once, so LocalSpots AI watches the closest ones and re-points as you move. Alerts need “Always” location access, which is optional.

Privacy

Your places are yours.

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your saved spots stay on your iPhone (and in your own iCloud if you use Pro), and your location never leaves your device.
