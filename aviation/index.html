The problem

Aviation parts don’t sell like anything else.

Most planning tools were built for products that sell every week. Aviation parts don’t, and that’s where those tools go wrong.

Demand is rare and lumpy

Most part numbers sell a handful of times a year, in bursts. Moving averages overstock some parts and run out of others.

Missing an AOG order is expensive

When an aircraft is grounded waiting on a part, the customer remembers who had it. A missed AOG order can cost the whole account.

Stock is costly to hold

A single rotable or LRU can tie up tens of thousands of dollars. A few wrong buys wipe out a year’s margin.

The real drivers get ignored

Fleet size, flight hours, removal rates and aircraft retirements predict demand far better than sales history alone.
